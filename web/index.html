How to play here

Translated by machine: tell us a better word.

The game runs in this tab, on your device. The mouse and the keys work as in the game. On the tactics board, drag a player with the right button to give him a run. On a Mac, Control and a click is the right button, and Control and a drag the right-button drag.

New here? Two short videos show how to play here and how to edit a saved game: the video guides on YouTube.

Your saved games

Save in the game as usual. The saves stay in this browser. Saved games downloads them as files and uploads them again, also to and from the desktop game, and its Edit button changes the players of a save: ability, attributes, positions, age.

Squad Hub

Squad Hub shows, scouts and changes the players of a saved game, or makes a data set for new games: your own copy of the database, where you change players and add new ones. Saved games lists the data sets: Use one for your next new game, download it as a zip, or upload one (an Update Team database, for example).

Membership

Membership holds Cloud Saves (each save goes to the cloud, and the game started on another computer or iPad gets it) and the Scout (in Squad Hub, a saved game's players by age, potential and attributes). Sign in with Google (Sign in, in the bar) to become a member. Pricing · Privacy (English)

As an app

Add the page to the home screen (on an iPad: Share, then Add to Home Screen) or install it from the address bar. It then opens without the browser's bars, and it starts without a connection once it has run.
