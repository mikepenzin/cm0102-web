What you pay for

Membership is a service: we store your own saved games and keep them the same on your devices (Cloud Saves), and the Scout in Squad Hub shows the players of your saved games that match your filters, the best 100 at a time (without Membership, the first 3). You do not pay for the game, which is free with or without Membership.

Levels

On Buy Me a Coffee, the Squad Player level is Membership. The Club Legend level is Membership and early access: new seasons in the database library and new features of the site come to Club Legends first, usually 14 days before everyone else. After that, what is free is free for everyone, and what is part of Membership is for every member. The Supporter level is a thank-you: it changes nothing in the game here. Each level has its own price: the Squad Player level is {{PRICE}}, the Club Legend level costs more, and Buy Me a Coffee shows every level's price before you join.

Why sign in with Google

Signing in is how your cloud saves know they are yours, on any device. Signing in alone changes nothing else: an account without Membership plays exactly like no account. We get your email address and name from Google, nothing else (Privacy).

Paying, cancelling, refunds

You become a member on Buy Me a Coffee, monthly, and it takes the payment. Join with the email of your Google account here, and Membership turns on by itself. Joined with another email? Write to us and we will link it.

The membership renews every month until you cancel. Cancel, pause or change it any time on Buy Me a Coffee: Membership then runs to the end of the month you paid for. A full refund within 14 days of any payment, no questions asked (Refunds).
