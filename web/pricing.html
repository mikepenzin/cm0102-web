Cloud Saves is a service: we store your own saved games and keep them the same on your devices. You do not pay for the game, which is free with or without Cloud Saves.

Why sign in with Google

Signing in is how your cloud saves know they are yours, on any device. Signing in alone changes nothing else: an account without Cloud Saves plays exactly like no account. We get your email address and name from Google, nothing else (Privacy).

Paying, cancelling, refunds
