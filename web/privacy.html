Privacy

CM 01/02 in the Browser is an unofficial fan project. The game runs in your browser, on your device.

Without an account

Nothing about you is kept on the server. The game files, your saved games and your settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. The server keeps no cookies and no log of what you do in the game.

With an account

You sign in with your Google account. Google tells this site:

Your Google account idA number that names your account; your saves are kept under it.
Your email addressTo show which account is signed in, and to give cloud saves to the accounts that have them.
Your name and pictureYour name is shown on the Account button. The picture is not used or kept.

The site asks Google for nothing else: not your contacts, your files or your mail. It keeps your account id, email address, name, the date you first and last signed in, and your plan.

Cloud saves

If your account has cloud saves, each saved game you make goes to the site's storage, with its file name, its size and the time it was saved. The storage is Tigris, an object store of Fly.io, where this site runs. Nothing else of the game goes there.

Cookies

A signed-in browser holds one cookie, cm_session, for 30 days: it says which account is signed in. During the sign-in a second cookie, cm_oauth, holds the state of the sign-in for at most 10 minutes. There are no other cookies, no ads and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Account, then Delete account, removes your account and all of your cloud saves from the storage at once. The saves in your browser stay until you clear the site's data in the browser. Signing out removes the cookie from the browser.
