# Music of the how-to videos

Two takes of "Matchday Manager", a track made with Suno for the how-to videos (web/README.md, "How-to videos"):

- `matchday-manager-app.mp3` (2:48): under `app.mp4`, How to play in your browser
- `matchday-manager-editor.mp3` (2:28): under `editor.mp4`, How to edit a saved game

`web/test/make-videos.mjs` takes them by default (`CM_VIDEO_MUSIC` and the like name another file). They are here so
the films can be made again; they are not part of the program, and the GPL of the code does not cover them. Their use
is as Suno's terms give it to the account that made them.
