The zones are named Zone 1 and Zone 2 in en Wikipedia, Zona 1 and Zona 2 in es Wikipedia and Group 1 and Group 2 in RSSSF, and the zone column holds 1 and 2. The tournament has 16 fechas, then a match between the two zone runners-up (third place and the Copa Libertadores order) and a final between the zone winners. Each zone has 15 clubs, so in fecha 1 to 11 and 13 to 16 each zone has 7 zone matches and one club at rest. The two resting clubs play each other in an inter-zone match, so every club plays one inter-zone match in its rest fecha and 14 zone matches, one per zone mate. Fecha 12 (22 to 25 April) is the clásicos round: it has no zone matches, and all 30 clubs play their rival in the other zone. Each club plays 16 matches (14 zone matches and 2 inter-zone matches), in agreement with RSSSF and es Wikipedia. All 15 pairs meet twice, home and away, against the fixed rival, so there is no draw match. In 11 pairs the rest fecha is first and fecha 12 reverses the venue. In the 4 other pairs fecha 12 is first and the rest fecha is 13 (Patronato at Rafaela), 14 (Atlético Tucumán at Belgrano), 15 (Sarmiento at Tigre) or 16 (San Martín at Godoy Cruz). The pairs, home sides and fechas are the same in RSSSF and es Wikipedia. en Wikipedia gives the format (14 zone matches and two matches against the rival, home and away) without a fecha list. The es Wikipedia text says the rival pairs are "mostly" classic rivalries, and every pair here is a rival pair in RSSSF. I did not check each pair against a rival list.
