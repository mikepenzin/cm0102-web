# Release 0.7.3
Status: In build

## Problem

Players reported faults in 0.7.1 and 0.7.2 on the forum. Some leagues still play places or club counts that differ from real life, and 0.7.2 held back the real places of five nations because they stopped the Northern Irish leagues.

## Solution

0.7.3 closes all 23 issues of milestone 0.7.3. League rules follow the rules table in the seasons where they still differ. Reported faults in the news, Save Game, history and competition screens are fixed at their cause. Squad numbers can go up to 99 with an option.

## User stories

1. As a player in Northern Ireland, I want the leagues to hold their real clubs and to start every season, so that a long game goes on after 2018.
2. As a player in Norway, Sweden, Brazil or Portugal, I want the real promotion and relegation places in the seasons before a count change.
3. As a player with Portugal or Spain, I want a 2001 start to pass the 2003/04 season change with no error box.
4. As a player in Croatia, Greece, Japan, Finland or Argentina, I want the club counts and places of the rules table.
5. As a player in MLS or the A-League, I want clubs to join and fold in their real seasons.
6. As a player in Sweden, I want the third tier in its real groups.
7. As a player in Belgium or the Netherlands, I want the real play-off rounds.
8. As a player in the Champions League, Europa League or Conference League, I want the league phase drawn from pots.
9. As a player, I want squad numbers up to 99 when I turn the option on, and the original game when I leave it off.
10. As a player, I want correct competition history, hosts and player nations.
11. As a national team manager, I want Save Game to suggest a name.
12. As a player in a long game, I want no "(null)" news, no error news and no error box from the news filter.
13. As a player, I want the competition arrow to list the right competitions.
14. As a human manager, I want a loan move to keep each player in one squad slot.
15. As a visitor to the site, I want the roadmap to show Done only for finished work.

## Decisions

Lead decisions, 18 November 2026. Max said on 13 November: decide with the recommended answer.

- L1. Scope stays whole. All 23 issues ship in 0.7.3.
- L2. With changing rules off, the game stays the original. The same-save set against the 0.7.2 build passes, except for a planned difference that a slice names.
- L3. Real life wins over the original's class values in a season game. The rules table is the source for counts and places.
- L4. Northern Ireland lands first. The duplicate split place of item 288 is fixed at its source, in the same slice as items 271 and 272.
- L5. The real places of slice 3 of the 0.7.2 league sizes spec land again from their branches, on the current base, with the Portuguese stop fix. The combined check of slices 1 and 2 plays Northern Ireland from 2001 and from 2018.
- L6. With Portugal's extra divisions off, the Segunda Liga's surplus goes to no division. No division exists below it in that game. The changelog names this limit.
- L7. A closed league loses a folded club to no division. A new club uses a club of the database when one exists, otherwise the real club record path of the season tables. Clubs join and fold only with changing rules on.
- L8. League phase pots apply from 2024/25 with changing rules on. The Champions League and Europa League use four pots of nine, and each club meets two clubs of each pot, one at home and one away. The Conference League uses six pots of six, and each club meets one club of each pot. A club never meets a club of its own nation and meets at most two clubs of another nation. Pots follow club reputation, highest first, because the game keeps no club coefficient. The entry code order of comparator 0x586f60 breaks a tie only. Other rounds keep their seeding.
- L9. Squad numbers follow issue 140. The option is "Squad numbers 1 to 99", off by default, with a row in the Options box. The save format does not change, and network games use the host's setting.
- L10. A national team manager's save name is the nation's name and the game date.
- L11. History, host and nation corrections apply at load in our own code, with a source for each row.
- L12. Issue 148: compare the same save on the original 3.9.68 game first. If the original does the same, the issue closes with the evidence and no code change.
- L13. Our own code only. No patch code of another modder and no data of another database maker.

- Screens: a smoke test that opens the screen and reads the result.
- Every soak plays through a World Cup, a European Championship and all season changes between.
- Release: the release set of the release tests spec.

## Out of scope

- Features of milestone 0.8.0.
- Issues outside milestone 0.7.3.
- New divisions other than the Swedish third tier groups.
